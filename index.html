READING 1 📖

The Travel Experience ✈️🛄

Traveling to a new country is always an adventure, but it can be difficult to understand how money works there. I have traveled to many different countries in my life, and I always find the local markets very interesting. Last summer, I visited a beautiful city in Europe. One of the first things I had to do was exchange my money for the local currency.

While walking through the city center, I found a traditional outdoor market. There were many people selling handmade items. I stopped at a small stall that sold leather bags. One specific product caught my eye—a dark brown backpack. At first, the price seemed a bit overpriced, and I wasn't sure if I should buy it. However, after looking at the quality of the leather and the stitching, I realized it was a real bargain. The seller told me it was handmade by his father. I decided that the bag was definitely worth the money because it would last for a very long time.

Have you ever bought something special like that while on vacation? For me, it felt like a small luxury because I usually don't buy expensive things. When I paid for the bag, the seller explained that the local tax was already included in the price. I gave him a large note, and he handed me my change in coins and smaller notes.

I have never seen such beautiful craftsmanship in a normal shopping mall. My best friend has visited that same market many times, and she always finds great things there too. I haven't spent much money on clothes this year, so I felt okay about this purchase. Buying something directly from the person who made it is a great experience. It makes the item feel more important than something from a big factory.

GRAMMAR 🔗

Present Perfect for past experiences

Meaning

We use the present perfect simple to talk about our experiences up to now. The time of the experiences is not important.

I have traveled to many countries.

We have seen that movie together. Don't you remember?

I've never been to China.

She has bought so many expensive things recently. Now her bank balance is near zero.

He hasn't played soccer but he has played basketball many times.

Although we do not give a specific time, we often use general time expressions like ever, never, before, in my life, so far, up until now with this use of the present perfect simple.

Form

Type Subject Auxiliary Past Participle
Affirmative (+) I / You / We / They have traveled.
Negative (-) He / She / It has not (hasn't) traveled.
Question (?) Have / Has Subject traveled?

Pronunciation

  • I have had many cars.
  • I've never been to Turkey.
  • Have you ever seen a fox? (Yes, I have. | No, I haven't.)
  • She's returned the laptop to the store.
  • He hasn't written any emails.
  • Has your brother ever driven a car?
  • My dog has eaten many things.
  • It has never touched my fish.
  • Has it ever been to the vet? (Yes, it has. | No, it hasn't.)
  • We have solved a lot of problems together.
  • We haven't had a fight up until now.
  • They've sold 52 apartments so far.
  • They haven't found a good employee.
  • Have they looked at the papers? (Yes, they have. | No, they haven't.)

READING 2 📖

A Lesson in Saving 💰📚

Managing money is a skill that everyone needs to learn. I have worked since I was eighteen years old, but I haven't always been good with my finances. When I was younger, I never checked my bank balance before going to the mall. I just bought whatever I wanted. Now that I am older, I understand that if you spend too much, you won't be able to afford the things you really need, like rent or electricity.

Last month, I saw a very expensive television in a shop window. I didn't have enough cash, so I decided to use my credit card. I knew this was a big expense, but I really wanted the TV for my new apartment. The shop assistant was very kind and told me they wouldn't charge me for delivery. He also gave me a 10% discount because it was the last one in the shop.

However, a week later, I started to feel worried. I realized that I didn't like the feeling of having to owe money to the bank. Have you ever felt stressed because of money? Being in debt is not a good experience, even if you have a nice new TV. I thought about it for two days and finally decided that I made a mistake.

I took the television back to the shop. Luckily, the store policy allowed me to return the item within thirty days if it was still in good condition. My brother has done the same thing before with a computer, so he helped me move it. After the shop gave me my money back, I felt much better. I have learned that it is better to save your money and wait until you can pay for something fully. Since that day, I haven't used my credit card for anything that isn't an emergency.

CONVERSATION 💬

Bank Account Problem 🏦

Clerk (Ms. Evans): Good morning, thank you for waiting. How can I help you today?

Customer (Mr. Davies): Good morning, Ms. Evans. I'm a bit worried. I checked my bank balance this morning, and it seems much too low. I think there’s been a charge I don't remember.

Clerk (Ms. Evans): I understand. Let me look at your account history. Ah, yes. I see a large payment here. It’s marked as an online expense from 'Fine Products Store.' Have you ever bought an expensive product from this store?

Customer (Mr. Davies): No, definitely not. I bought a new phone last month, but I paid for it using a different credit card. I haven't used this account for anything like that. I certainly can’t afford to lose this much money!

Clerk (Ms. Evans): I see. It appears the amount is £850. If you didn't agree to this, we need to treat it as a serious problem. Have you ever had a payment taken without your permission before, Mr. Davies?

Customer (Mr. Davies): No, never. I’ve always been very careful. If I don't get this sorted, I might be in debt by the end of the month! That money is worth a lot to me.

Clerk (Ms. Evans): Don't worry, we can fix this. I've looked at the details, and the payment seems to be a mistake made by the store. Sometimes their computer system incorrectly applies the full price instead of giving you a discount. It looks like they tried to charge you for something overpriced.

Customer (Mr. Davies): Oh, so I don't owe them the money?

Clerk (Ms. Evans): Correct. I have opened a dispute form right now. We will contact the 'Global Products Store' immediately and request a full return of the funds. I expect the money to be back in your balance within 48 hours. I’ve already contacted our team that deals with false payments.

Customer (Mr. Davies): That’s a relief. Thank you for sorting this out so quickly, Ms. Evans.

Clerk (Ms. Evans): You're very welcome, Mr. Davies. We will send you an email confirmation when the funds are fully returned. Is there anything else I can help you with today?

WRITING ✍️

Part 1: The Principles of a Complaint Letter

To write a professional complaint, follow these 5 core principles:

  • Be Polite but Firm: Use "I am disappointed" instead of "I am angry."
  • The "Experience" Strategy: Use the Present Perfect to show you are a loyal customer. (e.g., "I have shopped here for many years.")
  • Specify the Product: Name the product, purchase date, and payment method (credit/cash).
  • Explain the Value: Explain why it isn't worth the expense. Use words like overpriced.
  • State the Solution: Do you want a return, a discount, or a full refund to your balance?

Part 2: The Model Email

To: Customer Service Team
From: [Your Name]
Subject: Complaint regarding faulty tablet – Order #12345
Dear Customer Service Team, I am writing to complain about a tablet I bought from your store last week. The price was $300, which I thought was a bargain during your sale. However, I am very disappointed with the quality. I have bought many electronics from your company in the past, and I have always been satisfied with your service. However, this specific product stopped working after only two days. The battery does not charge at all. I think the item is overpriced because it does not work as promised. I have never had such a bad experience with a new purchase before. This was a large expense for me, and I cannot afford to keep a broken tablet. Because I owe money on my credit card for this purchase, I am very worried about my bank balance. I would like to return the tablet to your store. Could you please provide a full refund, including the tax I paid? Have you ever had this problem with other customers? I look forward to your reply and a quick solution.
Yours faithfully, [Your Name]
